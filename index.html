Scrum-poker.live was a real-time task estimation tool for agile teams. Players could create a room, invite the team, vote on story points and see the results together.

The live service is offline for now, so rooms can't be created. Below are screenshots of the working app, its main features and the tech stack behind it.

Features

What the app could do before it was paused.

5

Real-time voting

Votes, reveals and player joins sync instantly for everyone over WebSockets.

8

Issue backlog

Add, edit and pick issues in the side panel. Each one keeps its final estimate.

13

Instant statistics

Most chosen value, average and agreement level are calculated when cards are revealed.

21

Custom card decks

Pick the voting options, turn on auto reveal and protect a room with a password.

34

Emoji reactions

React with emoji, or throw a paper ball at a teammate who is taking too long.

55

Guest accounts

Join with just a name. JWT authentication keeps the session across refreshes.
